Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Anikoto (the "app"), published by Youssef Ouadelli ("we", "us"). We keep it short because the app is simple.

The short version: there are no accounts, we don't run a server that stores your data, and your favorites stay on your device. The app talks to a few third-party services to show anime information and ads. Details below.

1. What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. You don't create an account. We don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your favorites list is saved in a file on your iPhone. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Cached images are kept temporarily so screens load faster.

3. Third-party services the app talks to

Anime information — AniList. To show titles, artwork, cast, staff and episodes, the app sends requests to AniList and its image servers. These requests contain what's needed to answer them — for example the text you search for or the identifier of the series you open — and, as with any internet request, your IP address is visible to AniList. AniList operates under its own privacy policy.

Advertising — Google AdMob. The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, such as your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Tracking permission (iOS). The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The app works the same either way; if you decline, ads are simply less personalized.
  • Consent form (EEA, UK and some U.S. states). Where the law requires it, the app shows a consent form before requesting ads. You can review or change your choices later in the app under Settings › Privacy Choices.
  • Apple's ad settings. You can also limit personalized ads in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.

5. Children

Anikoto is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given personal information to a third-party service through the app, contact us and we'll help you reach the right party.

6. Security

The app connects to its services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to breach.

7. International transfers

The third-party services above may process information in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards and policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to access or delete; to exercise those rights with Google or AniList, use their privacy tools. We don't sell personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
