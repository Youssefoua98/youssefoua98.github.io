Frequently asked questions

Can I watch anime in Anikoto?

No. Anikoto is an information and discovery guide — it doesn't stream or download video. Use it to find what to watch and learn about it; where official links are available they open in your browser.

Where does the anime information come from?

From AniList, including titles, artwork, cast, staff and episode information. If something looks wrong or missing, it comes from that database and is best corrected at the source.

Are my favorites synced between devices?

No. Favorites are saved only on the iPhone you use and are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Why does the app show ads?

Anikoto is free, and ads pay for it. You can change your tracking choice in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and in the app under Settings › Privacy Choices where it's shown.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The app is available in English and French and follows your iPhone's language.

An ad or a page looks broken — what should I do?

Please email us with your iPhone model, iOS version and what you saw (a screenshot helps). We read every message.
