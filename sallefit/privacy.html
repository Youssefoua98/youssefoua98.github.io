Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version: SalleFit has no account and no server. Your workouts, water, sleep and weight entries are stored only on your iPhone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Details below.

Who we are

SalleFit is made by Youssef Ouadelli, an independent developer. Contact: youssef.ouadelli1998@gmail.com.

Data you enter in the app

Your first name, fitness goal, level and focus areas, completed workouts and challenge progress, water intake, sleep times and how you felt, weight and optional waist and hip measurements are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We don't operate a server that stores user data and we can't see it.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

SalleFit does not use HealthKit, your camera, your photos, your contacts or your precise location.

Advertising — Google AdMob

SalleFit is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them;
  • crash, performance and diagnostic data from the ad software.

Your workout, water, sleep and weight data are never shared with Google. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: you can allow or refuse tracking when the app asks, and change it any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you refuse, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): Google's consent form lets you choose how ads are personalised. You can reopen it from Profile › Ad privacy choices.
  • Deleting your data: Profile › Delete all my data erases everything the app stored. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Inappropriate ads: use Profile › Report an ad, or email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to access or delete; to exercise those rights with Google, use Google's privacy tools. We don't sell personal information.

Children

SalleFit is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
