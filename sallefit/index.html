What's inside

Guided home workouts

Short sessions with an illustrated coach, a timer and voice cues. No equipment needed.

Challenges

Day-by-day programs that build a routine, from a gentle start to more energetic sessions.

Water tracker

Log glasses and bottles in one tap and see your last 7 days.

Sleep tracker

Log last night, note how you felt and get a gentle bedtime reminder.

Weight tracker

Weigh-ins with optional waist and hip measurements, shown as a simple trend.

Private by design

No account. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

SalleFit offers general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Check with a health professional before starting a new routine, especially if you are pregnant, injured or have a medical condition.
