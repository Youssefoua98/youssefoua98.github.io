FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and nothing to log in to.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. We never receive it.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile › Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes everything.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data is included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup on a new phone brings it with it.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep SalleFit free. You can control personalisation with the iOS tracking setting and, where available, Profile › Ad privacy choices.

I saw an inappropriate ad.

Use Profile › Report an ad, or email us with the time and screen where you saw it, and we'll block it.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications for SalleFit are allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications, and that the reminder is switched on in the app.
