Privacy Policy

FootScore for iPhone · Effective October 26, 2026

In short: FootScore requires no account and does not collect your name, email or location. Your favorites stay on your phone. The app is free thanks to Google AdMob advertising, which may use your device's advertising identifier depending on your choices.

1. Who we are

FootScore ("the app", "we") is a sports scores app for iPhone. This policy explains what information is processed and why. Questions: youssef.ouadelli1998@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no password.
  • No name, email, phone number or address.
  • No location, contacts or photos.
  • We do not run any server that receives your personal data.

3. Data stored on your device

Your favorite teams and competitions, followed matches and a few settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app. Match reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device: nothing is sent to a server.

4. Sports data

To show scores, the app queries third-party sports data providers. These requests contain only what is needed (for example the requested date or competition) plus the technical information inherent to any internet connection, such as your IP address, which these providers may log under their own policies.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FootScore displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow it through iOS's "Allow Tracking" prompt;
  • ad-related usage and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, performance);
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address.

If you decline tracking, non-personalized ads are shown. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form is shown and you can change your choices at any time in the app under About (ⓘ) → Ad choices. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more: How Google uses data.

7. Children

FootScore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side: deleting the app erases all local data. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights with Google. For any request: youssef.ouadelli1998@gmail.com.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
