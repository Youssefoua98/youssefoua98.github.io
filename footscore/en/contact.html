FAQ

A competition is missing? Some leagues are not yet published by our data sources; they are marked "Coming soon" (« Bientôt disponible »).

How do I change my ad choices? In the app: Competitions → ⓘ → Ad choices, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app: your favorites and settings are stored only on your phone.

Which time zone are match times in? Always your device's own time zone.
